Signature Society

Email platform comparison

Two platforms are worth considering for the Signature Society store: Omnisend and Klaviyo. Both connect natively to Shopify, both pull in order data, and both handle the flows we plan to build. The differences come down to cost as the list grows, how much of the platform you will realistically use, and how easy it is to manage day to day.

The short version

Omnisend costs less at every list size, and the gap widens as the list grows. Under 2,500 contacts the difference is roughly $15 a month, which is small enough that it should not decide this on its own. Past 25,000 contacts the difference becomes real money.

Neither platform offers an annual discount. Both bill monthly and both raise your price automatically when your contact count crosses into the next band.

Higher tiers

If you outgrow the entry plan
Omnisend Pro

From $59 per month

  • Requires a minimum of 2,500 contacts
  • Removes the monthly send cap entirely
  • Unlocks advanced reporting and priority support
  • Required if you ever want to send SMS
  • Not needed at launch
Klaviyo Email plus Mobile

About $15 more per month

  • Adds SMS, MMS and WhatsApp at any contact tier
  • Same email features as the base plan
  • Message credits are bundled, then billed per use
  • Add-ons for reviews and advanced analytics are priced separately
  • Above 250,000 contacts, billing moves to a custom quote

Recommendation

For Signature Society

Start on Omnisend Standard. At the list size we expect at launch, the cost difference is minor, so the decision comes down to what your team touches after the site goes live. Omnisend keeps contacts in one straightforward list, and the template editor is simple enough that swapping an image, updating copy and sending a campaign takes a few minutes without a marketing background.

Klaviyo is the stronger platform if email becomes a dedicated channel with someone managing it regularly. The predictive segmentation and deeper reporting are genuinely better. They are also features that go unused on most stores in their first year, and the platform asks more of whoever is running it.

Either way, the flows and templates we build carry over. Moving between platforms later means rebuilding the designs, so it is worth choosing based on the next twelve months rather than the next five years.

A note on these numbers. Both platforms adjust pricing regularly and published figures vary between sources at the larger list sizes. The tiers above reflect US pricing as of August 2026 and should be confirmed directly with each platform before you commit. Pricing is based on stored contacts on both platforms, including contacts who have unsubscribed, so keeping the list clean directly affects the monthly bill.
