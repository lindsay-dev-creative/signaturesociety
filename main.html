Signature
Society

Brand guidelines covering the typographic system and the color system. Every specimen on this page is rendered in the live token it documents, so what you see is what ships. Resize the window to watch the responsive scale change.

Typefaces

Kepler Std carries the brand voice and is used at four optical sizes. Choosing the optical size by point size is not optional: it is what keeps the letterforms correct at scale. PP Playground sits above all of it as the hero face. Inter is the secondary face, used for interface copy, forms and the form button.

Kepler Std across seven heading steps and three body steps, with PP Playground above it for hero moments. Three ratios rather than one: desktop is 18px base at 1.25, tablet 17px at 1.2, mobile 17px at 1.125. Only the headings, the hero steps and the body base move; sm and xs are fixed everywhere. Where the optical size changes

The 32px boundary does not sit between two fixed steps, because all three ratios differ. heading-2x is 35px on desktop and 24px on mobile, so it starts in Display and ends in Subhead. heading-3x and heading-4x cross the same line at mobile only. The CSS handles this with family overrides in the breakpoints, so the class name stays the same and the correct cut is chosen for you.

Color system

Six families running 900 at the darkest to 50 at the lightest. Five carry ten steps; Neutral carries eleven, with an extra 950 below the 900. The brand swatch is marked with an inset rule and a bullet, and it is not the same step in every family: 800 for Colors 1, 2, 3 and 4, 400 for Color 5, 200 for Neutral. Each family heading names its own. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Action required · confirm Pantone separation

All four dark families sit low in lightness at their brand step. Before anything goes to a printer, run the six brand values through Illustrator with the Solid Coated library loaded and confirm all six come back distinct. Below roughly 12 percent lightness the library collapses most dark colors onto Black 6 C regardless of hue, and Color 1 at #08142B is the one most exposed to that.

The ramps are low chroma throughout. color1/500 is #5671A5, a muted slate blue that still reads as the brand, so the earlier warning to avoid the 400 to 600 band on links and focus states no longer applies.

Color 2 fills the secondary role in the brand. The families are named color1 through color5 in Figma and are called the same here, and the step numbers now match the Figma scales frame one for one, so the document and the design file cannot drift apart.

Two things to fix in the Figma file, carried here as they stand rather than corrected silently. color2/700 holds #4D0F19, identical to color2/800, so the burgundy ramp has no distinct 700 and a hover state built on it will not read as a change. Separately, the color5 900 and 800 layers are labelled the other way round from every other family; they are recorded here in lightness order, with 900 as the darker of the two.
